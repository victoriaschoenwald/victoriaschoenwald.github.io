Hello! I am **Victoria Schoenwald**, an atmospheric scientist and Postdoctoral Researcher in the Kirtman Research Group at the University of Miami Rosenstiel School of Marine, Atmospheric, and Earth Science.

My research explores how climate change and natural variability influence regional sea-level rise and coastal flooding along the U.S. East Coast. I am especially interested in the connections among atmospheric forcing, Gulf Stream variability, coastal ocean dynamics, and large-scale climate patterns.

I use observations, satellite data, ocean and atmospheric reanalyses, and climate-model experiments to better understand why sea level changes differently from one coastal region to another. My broader goal is to make climate information more useful and accessible for the communities affected by coastal change.

## Current research

My current work focuses on separating long-term trends from natural variability in coastal sea level and identifying the atmospheric and oceanic processes behind those changes. I also work on regional climate variability, high-resolution climate-model analysis, and science communication.

Outside of research, I enjoy traveling, hiking, yoga, and spending time near the ocean.
